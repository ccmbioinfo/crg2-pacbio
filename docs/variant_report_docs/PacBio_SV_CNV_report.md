# Annotating and filtering SV and CNV calls from PacBio HiFi sequencing

Madeline Couse, Christian Marshall, Kristin Kernohan, Giulia del Gobbo, Neta Pipko, Rohan Khan

**Version 2026-02**

## Changelog

Changes from 2026-02 version: see ‘HiFiCNV genotypes’ section

Summary

SV reports are generated from joint-genotyped pbsv or sawfish VCFs. CNV reports are generated from HiFiCNV VCFs, or sawfish VCFs. CNVs generated by HiFiCNV are not joint-genotyped. Instead, the [crg2-pacbio](https://github.com/ccmbioinfo/crg2-pacbio) pipeline merges single-sample HiFiCNV calls into a multi-sample VCF using bcftools, then collapses similar CNVs (50% reciprocal overlap and maximum reference distance of 4000bp) using [truvari](https://github.com/ACEnglish/truvari).

## C4R and TG databases of SV calls from PacBio HiFi data

- bcftools merge to combine cohort pbsv vcfs (\*noting that I had to filter out non-PASS variants from the TG database because Truvari complained about these)
- [Truvari](http://null/) collapse to merge similar SVs
  - Insertions
    - SV position must be within 50 bp
    - Length overlap is at least 80%
    - Percent sequence similarity must be at least 80%
  - Deletions, duplications, and inversions
    - SV position must be within 500 bp
    - Reciprocal overlap is at least 80%
- Note that in report generation, an SV may appear to be absent from the TG database; in some cases, this can be because the SV was collapsed into a representative SV by truvari. For example, similar insertions within a 50 bp window are merged into one representative SV record by truvari, which selects the coordinates of the most common insertion.
- Currently, the C4R database is composed of 139 C4R participants, with 2-4 participants per family sequenced.
- Currently, the TG database is composed of 217 TG participants from DECODER and geneSTEPS, with 2-4 participants per family sequenced.

## C4R and TG databases of CNV calls from PacBio HiFi data

- bcftools merge to combine cohort HiFiCNV vcfs
- [Truvari](http://null/) collapse to merge similar CNVs
  - Deletions and duplications
    - CNV position must be within 4000 bp; the CNV coordinates called by HiFiCNV have 2000bp confidence windows on either side.
    - Reciprocal overlap is at least 50%
- Currently, the C4R database is composed of 139 C4R participants, with 2-4 participants per family sequenced.
- Currently, the TG database is composed of 217 TG participants from DECODER and geneSTEPS, with 2-4 participants per family sequenced.

## HiFiCNV genotypes

Note that natively, [HiFICNV genotypes](https://github.com/PacificBiosciences/HiFiCNV/issues/32) are placeholders. Instead of reporting these placeholders, we use the copy number that HiFiCNV reports to provide a more informative genotype based on the guidelines outlined by [DRAGEN](https://help.dragen.illumina.com/dragen-v4.3/product-guide/dragen-v4.3/dragen-dna-pipeline/cnv-calling/cnv-output#note-on-genotype-annotation-in-germline-copy-number-calling).

| **Ploidy** | **SVTYPE** | **Copy number** | **Genotype** | **Zygosity** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Diploid | DUP | \>2 | ./1 | het or hom |
| Diploid | DEL | 0 | 1/1 | hom |
| Diploid | DEL | 1 | 0/1 | het |
| Haploid | DUP | \>1 | 1 | hom |
| Haploid | DEL | 0 | 1 | hom |
| Diploid or Haploid | DEL or DUP | . | ./. | ./. |

## Recommended filters for analysis

- CoLoRSdb_maxAF \< 0.01
- gnomad_maxAF \< 0.01
- DGV_maxAF \< 0.01
- C4R_nhomalt_max = 0
- TG_nhomalt_max = 0

For analysis, we recommend considering any exon-overlapping SVs seen in proband,

- *probandID*.zyg uncheck - and ./.
- EXONS_SPANNED uncheck 0

And also any genic SV either in OMIM and/or in genes matching HPO terms

- omim_phenotype uncheck .
- HPO uncheck nan

**Other analysis considerations:**

- The positions of insertions/deletions within short tandem repeat (STR) regions are not always exact, and additionally, these can have variable sizes within the population. The “rarity” filters may not necessarily filter out some of these polymorphic regions depending on the exact position of an insertion/deletion in the control cohorts we use. Consider the Repeat_type_left and Repeat_type_right column when interpreting variants – small insertions/deletions in STRs may not be terribly compelling if there are other insertions/deletions noted in these regions in controls. STR expansions will be better assessed in the TRGT analysis files where STR alleles are genotyped and compared to a population of controls to identify outlying repeat expansions.
- Also consider whether a region is part of a “dark region” or ENCODE blacklist region that has historically had poor sequencing coverage and is therefore not well represented in short-read control datasets. The columns related to PB_dark_region, PB_odd_region, ENCODE_blacklist_characteristics help with this.
- PacBio recommends not putting too much weight in the genotype of SVs as heterozygous vs homozygous. We recommend mainly considering presence/absence of the variant in each sample, and if you have a strong candidate that is called as homozygous, confirm zygosity using read support (*sampleID*\_AD and *sampleID*\_DP), and checking BAM file if necessary.
- Currently, the *sampleID*\_**GT** column reports on the variant genotypes as **phased genotypes**. 0 is reference, 1 is alt. A “/” is used when the genotype was not able to be phased, and “\|” is used for designating the phase. So ./. is a missing genotype (poor quality/no coverage to call; 0/1, 0\|1, 1\|0, etc are heterozygous genotypes; 0/0 is homozygous reference; and 1//1 is homozygous alt. The *sampleID*\_**PS** column reports on the phase set ID within which each variant resides. This corresponds to a block of the genome wherein variants could be phased to one haplotype or the other – if you have two variants that are annotated to the same phase set, you can therefore interpret their phased genotypes to determine whether they are in cis (e.g GT at SV1 = 0\|1 and SV2 = 0\|1) or in trans (e.g. GT at SV1 = 0\|1 and SV2 = 1\|0).
- If the `GENE_NAME` or `ENSEMBL_GENE` value approaches the Excel cell limit, it is continued in dynamically created columns such as `GENE_NAME_2` or `ENSEMBL_GENE_2`. The complete gene list is retained in both intermediate and final CSV reports.
